Poston Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Poston ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our service, which integrates with the TikTok API to manage and automate TikTok account operations.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Poston, we may collect the following types of information:

  • TikTok Account Information: Basic account information obtained through TikTok API authentication (user ID, display name) via the user.info.basic scope.
  • Video Analytics Data: Video performance metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) retrieved from your TikTok accounts via the video.list scope.
  • Uploaded Content: Videos and photo carousels that you choose to upload from Google Drive to TikTok via the video.upload scope.
  • Google Account Information: Access to Google Drive and Google Spreadsheet is required to store analytics data and retrieve content for upload.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate and connect your TikTok accounts to the service.
  • To automatically retrieve and record video analytics data at scheduled intervals.
  • To upload designated content from Google Drive to your TikTok accounts.
  • To provide multi-account management functionality for business use.

3. Data Storage and Security

All analytics data collected from TikTok is stored in your own Google Spreadsheet, which is accessible only to you and authorized users you designate. We do not store your TikTok credentials or personal data on external servers beyond what is required for the Google Apps Script to function.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Third-Party Services

Poston integrates with the following third-party services:

  • TikTok API: Governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Apps Script / Google Drive / Google Sheets: Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or TikTok account data with any third parties, except as required by law or as necessary to provide the service (e.g., TikTok API and Google services as described above).

6. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained in your Google Spreadsheet for as long as you choose to keep it. You may delete the data at any time. Access tokens for TikTok accounts are stored within your Google Apps Script project and can be revoked at any time through TikTok's account settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data collected about your TikTok accounts.
  • Request deletion of any stored data.
  • Revoke TikTok API access at any time via your TikTok account settings.
  • Disconnect Google account access at any time via your Google account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Poston is intended for business use and is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
