1. Description of Service

Poston is a Google Apps Script-based tool that integrates with the TikTok API to help businesses manage multiple TikTok accounts. The Service provides the following features:

  • Automatic retrieval of video analytics data (views, likes, comments, shares) from up to 5 TikTok accounts.
  • Scheduled data logging to Google Spreadsheet every 30 minutes.
  • Uploading of videos and photo carousels from Google Drive to TikTok accounts via the TikTok Content Posting API.
